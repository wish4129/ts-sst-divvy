## 3. Structural Shifts

### Market Dynamics
- **Bursa Malaysia** positioned as ASEAN "safe harbour" as investors flee Indonesia (reported Feb 2026)
- **FBM KLCI** projected for takeoff after volatile 2025 (NST, Dec 2025)
- Foreign investors "cool on Malaysian stocks, hot on fixed income" (The Edge, Jul 2025 — pattern persists into 2026)
- **May 2026**: Foreign investors extended selling streak — RM912M exited in one week
- **March 2026**: RM725M withdrawn in a single week
- The selling is broad-based (5 consecutive weeks) but the pace has slowed (from RM230M/day to RM78M/day by Friday)

### Local vs Foreign Dynamics
- Local institutions are the **sole net buyers**, absorbing foreign selling for 9 consecutive weeks
- Without local institutional support, KLCI would be significantly lower
- Retail participation is declining (-23% volume WoW) — retail enthusiasm for new IPOs remains but secondary market trading is cooling
